# Health-guidance boundaries and references

Updated November 27, 2026. This is a source-informed engineering review, not clinician approval. SVASA is not a medical device. The profile rules are conservative product heuristics, not a validated risk model. Forecast windows cannot certify safe exposure.

- WHO 2021 global air quality guidelines: https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240034228 — compare matching averaging periods and units; guideline values are population-health references, not individual safety thresholds.
- CDC wildfire smoke and chronic conditions: https://www.cdc.gov/wildfires/risk-factors/wildfire-smoke-and-people-with-chronic-conditions.html — particle protection, indoor exposure reduction and heat considerations. Adapted cautiously for ambient particle pollution; wildfire-specific guidance is not a substitute for local advice.
- CDC preparing for wildfires: https://www.cdc.gov/wildfires/safety/index.html — children two years and older may use appropriately fitted masks; do not mask infants. Respirators do not protect against gases.
- EPA children and smoke: https://www.epa.gov/children/protect-children-wildfires-smoke-and-volcanic-ash — prioritize cleaner indoor spaces and safe cooling over relying on adult-sized respirators.

Clinical review and native-language review remain pending. No claim of reduced illness or measured inhaled dose is made.
