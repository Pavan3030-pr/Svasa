How to read these numbers

US AQI is supplied by the atmospheric model. Pollutant-specific averaging periods differ. The optional India-scale number is a partial model estimate using at least three of six available pollutants, including particulate matter. Four pollutants use at least 16 hourly samples over 24 hours; ozone and CO require all eight samples over eight hours. Ammonia and lead are unavailable for India in this model, so the estimate may understate the full index. It is not an official CPCB AQI.

The pollutant concentrations below are current hourly readings, so they can look higher or lower than the AQI, which smooths over the day.

Data source: Open-Meteo / Copernicus CAMS atmospheric model. These are modelled estimates for a grid cell, not readings from a monitor on your street.
