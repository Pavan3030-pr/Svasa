Built to be trusted

Honest about what it knows.

Honest data

Numbers come from the Open-Meteo / Copernicus CAMS atmospheric model. They are estimates for an area, not a monitor on your street, and the app says so.

Safety first

Words like “chest pain” or “I cannot breathe” skip the AI and show a clear message to contact emergency services. Svasa gives general guidance, not medical advice.

Private by design

No account, no tracking. Your profile and recent cities stay in your own browser.
